# Midterm Reflection and Self-Assessment

Jackson Horan — COMP 363, Fall 2026

## Attendance

So far this term I have missed two class meetings which is well under the
threshold noted in the course policies. The first absence came when my roommate
tested positive for COVID and I started feeling sick myself. Rather than risk
bringing COVID into the classroom, I stayed home as a precaution. The second
absence was because of a review meeting at my internship. I had spent a
considerable amount of time building a feature for the team, and the review was
my chance to present it to the company and demo the tool for my colleagues. To
make up for both absences, I read through the meeting notes in conjunction with
the week's assignment and self-studied any areas that I found troubling.

## Homework submission

I have submitted every homework assignment this term, all of them on time, and I
have not missed any. Even in the weeks I missed class, I made sure the
assignment still went in by the deadline, since keeping up with the homework is
how I stay current with the material when I can't be in the room.

## Adherence to feedback

The only feedback I have received so far came after the first week's assignment,
and it pointed out that the variable names in my testing code were not
descriptive enough. I have been more deliberate about naming variables in my
tests so that it is clear what each one holds and what is being checked, and
that issue has not come up again. I have not received any other feedback.

## Time spent outside class

Outside of class meetings, I spend about four hours a week on this course,
reading, working through the material, and completing assignments. Being honest,
four hours is below the six hours a week the course sets as the mark of strong
engagement, and it is the area where I have the most room to grow in the second
half of the term.

## Class participation and engagement

Overall, I would describe my engagement in class as good. I answer some
questions and I stay focused on the lecture for most of the meeting. My laptop
does pull me away occasionally, though usually not toward anything unrelated to
the course. More often, a term or topic mentioned in lecture catches my interest
and I end up down a rabbit hole looking it up. I think I could be somewhat more
engaged, and I do want to be. For the rest of the term, I plan to jot down
anything I want to explore and look it up after class instead of during it, and
to offer answers more often, even when I am not certain I have them exactly
right.

## Proposed midterm grade

I propose a midterm grade of A. I have missed only two class meetings, I have
submitted every assignment on time, I acted on the feedback I received, and I
stay engaged in lecture. I also have a concrete plan for the second half of the
term, both to sharpen my focus in class and to put in more consistent time
outside of it, working toward the six-hour mark. I believe the overall picture
reflects A-level engagement with the course.
